About Me

Hello! I'm John Anniluv A. Atag, a dedicated IT student and commissioned artist based in Naga City. I have 2 years of experience creating clean, expressive traditional and digital illustrations.

I'm a strong communicator, skilled in client negotiations and advertising, ensuring clear collaboration and effective promotion of my work. I combine creativity and technical knowledge to deliver consistent, high-quality visual outputs.

Experienced in digital illustration using Medibang Paint and IbisPaint X, specializing in clean linework, expressive styles, and original character art.

Currently taking up IT at STI Naga City, I'm passionate about blending art and technology to build meaningful digital experiences.
